Governance is for teams that need a control plane in front of every model call — not a brochure.

## Trust Center

**Dashboard → Governance** is the Trust Center: models under review, open violations, pending approvals. Search filters those lists.

## Policies

**Policies** are stored rules (model glob, roles, action). They run on the gateway. Seed defaults from the page when your org has none.

## Approvals

A model can sit in `pending` until someone approves it. The sidebar badge and inbox both read pending `model_approvals`.

## Violations

Failed policy checks write `policy_violations`. Unresolved rows show in the inbox. Resolve them from **Violations**.

## Compliance & sector packs

Compliance controls and sector templates (finance, health, public sector) apply packs of policies you can attach to the org. Evidence notes are stored on the control rows.

## Audit

**Audit logs** (workspace and site-admin) record key, billing, and impersonation actions. The inbox also surfaces the latest org audit lines.

None of these pages ship with “5 open items” hardcoded. If the badge is empty, you have zero open rows.
